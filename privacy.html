NFC Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

NFC Toolkit ("the app") lets you read, write and manage NFC tags on your iPhone.

Data stays on your device

  • Tags you scan, your scan history and your saved library are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no user accounts, and we never receive this data.
  • Data is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app, other than as described under "Purchases" below. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

NFC

The app uses your iPhone's NFC reader only when you tap a scan or write action. Tag contents are processed on the device.

No advertising

Since version 1.3, NFC Toolkit shows no ads and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Free users can try each Pro tool once instead; which tools you've tried is remembered only on your device (in the iOS Keychain).

Versions 1.0 to 1.1.1 offered optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, which could collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allowed tracking), IP address and ad interaction data. If you still use one of those versions, you can decline tracking when iOS asks, or update the app to remove the ads entirely. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
